- **Industry Context:** Designed specifically for the FMCG and Food Processing sector to handle heavy-duty seed crushing and oil extraction processes.
- **Core Operation:** Implemented comprehensive automated sequential control using Siemens S7-1200 PLC to ensure continuous material flow without manual intervention. 
- **Motor Control:** Integrated Multi-Brand VFDs to regulate the speed of large induction motors dynamically based on the load and processing stage.
- **Safety & Wiring:** Developed robust relay logic and MCC panel interlocking to protect equipment from overload and ensure factory safety standards.
